Accuracy of content

We take care to source every historical statement, and the app's Sources panel lists where each fact comes from. History is nonetheless a matter of scholarship, and figures such as casualties and even battle names vary between sources. The battle layer is maintained by a third party and may change. Present-day state boundaries are used on the map. If you find an error, please tell us so it can be corrected; do not rely on the app as a sole authority for graded work or publication.
